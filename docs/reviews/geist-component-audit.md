# Geist-informed component audit

Railgrid’s components are evaluated against official Geist guidance as a reference, while preserving Violet Circuit and decisions already approved in the walkthrough. This is a work ledger, not a claim that unchanged components have been verified. A component is marked complete only after its relevant states, behavior, product use, and available evidence have been checked; partial findings do not establish full conformance.

Reference: [Geist introduction](https://vercel.com/geist/introduction). Status: **comparison pass complete**, with ResourceBackLink deferred and verification limits recorded below.

## Findings from this comparison

- **ActionMenu:** added printable-key navigation and skipped disabled actions; preserved Railgrid’s transparent trigger and violet focus.
- **Route tabs:** added native link support for provider routes while keeping buttons for local choices, preserving modified-click navigation.
- **Progress and interruptions:** made deletion progress announcements less repetitive and kept App Studio clarification actions focused while pending.
- **AI activity:** kept disclosure targets stable while their content stays lazy.
- **ResourceBoard:** enlarged the empty-column collapse target for coarse pointers without changing its desktop geometry.
- **Skill catalog:** made no-match feedback identify the query and explain how to clear it, with bounded copy for long queries.
- **Impact relationships:** defaulted narrow screens to the named list and moved the graph legend into Graph, making relations easier to reach without depending on line colors.

These findings cover the Geist-informed pass, not every change on the branch. The table records narrower evidence and known limitations per component. Fixture checks use simulated data; they do not certify live services, every product caller, assistive technology or all browsers. ResourceBackLink remains deferred at the user’s request. The relationship graph has a documented remaining color-vision distinction concern despite its labeled list alternative.
